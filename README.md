## 📌 Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive academic summary and reference guide for the course **Human Vision: A Computational Approach (76921)** taught at **The Hebrew University of Jerusalem**. 

The course explores the intersection of computational neuroscience, biological visual processing, and computer vision algorithms—modeling how the human brain transforms raw light signals into structured visual perceptions.

---

## 📄 Summary Topics Covered

The summary synthesizes mathematical foundations, biological neural mechanisms, and computational frameworks, including:

* **Image Formation & Early Vision:** Optics, photoreceptors, visual acuity, and spatial frequency filtering.
* **Retinal Processing & Lateral Inhibition:** Receptive fields, Difference of Gaussians (DoG), and edge detection models.
* **Primary Visual Cortex (V1):** Orientation selectivity, simple/complex cells, Gabor filters, and cortical architecture.
* **Motion & Depth Perception:** Optical flow, binocular stereo vision, multi-view geometry, and disparity computation.
* **Object Recognition & High-Level Vision:** Ventral/dorsal streams, feature integration, and visual attention mechanisms.
